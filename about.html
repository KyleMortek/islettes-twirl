About us

Islettes' Elite Dance Twirl Team

Baton twirling is a blend of artistry and athleticism. We've been teaching it in Western New York for generations of twirlers.

Who we are

A team, and a family commitment

Islettes' Elite offers four units based on age and ability, from Pre-K through college. Every unit competes in local NBTA and Twirling Unlimited competitions, and every unit marches in local parades and performances.

Across our generations the team has proudly earned over 20 National Titles — and every generation brings its own strengths and spirit. We don't compare our team to others; each has its own goals and training focus.

Being part of Islettes' Elite is a family commitment. Time and dedication are what make a twirler successful, and families who stay engaged and supportive make the whole season work.

What we expect of each other

Ambassadors of WNY

Members of Islettes' Elite represent Western New York. Twirlers and their families model positive behaviour at all times — at the gym, at events, at competitions, and online.

Good sportsmanship

We cheer loudly for our girls and always applaud other teams, modelling Christian sportsmanship and positivity.

Respect, always

We do not tolerate bullying, gossip, foul language, or discrimination. We expect everyone — twirlers and parents — to support a healthy, respectful environment.

Safety first

Staff are positioned throughout the group at parades, hydration is monitored, and twirlers are released only when a staff member personally hands them to you. Your child's safety is always our top priority.

Practice = Progress

Twirlers arrive prepared and properly dressed, keep their team folder organised, practise at home, and stay positive. You can do hard things.

Be your twirler's biggest fan

Parents bring their twirler on time and prepared, check the folder after every practice, and pitch in with the Parent Support Team at parades.

Relax, have fun, enjoy the journey

Baton twirling is a subjective sport, with many factors beyond our control. This is not a life-or-death contest — it's a celebration of skill, teamwork, and joy.
