Our four units

Twirlers are placed by grade and ability. Every unit competes in local NBTA and TU competitions, and every unit marches in our parades.

Starlettes

Pre-K & Kindergarten

Our youngest twirlers learn the fundamentals of baton and movement, and perform alongside the whole team.

Shooting Stars

1st Grade

Building on the basics with longer routines and a first taste of competition floor time.

Rising Stars

2nd – 5th Grade

More demanding choreography, with twirlers expected to arrive knowing the previous week's material.

Starfire

6th Grade through College

Our senior unit carries the most advanced routines and mentors their younger “sisters” at every event.

Practice = Progress

Twirlers practice at home between sessions and come to each class with the previous week's material memorized. Results reflect the time and commitment each athlete puts in.

We cheer for everyone

We cheer loudly for our girls and always applaud other teams. Every team has its own goals and training focus, and good sportsmanship is non-negotiable — for twirlers and families alike.
