How to pay

We accept:

  • Cash
  • Check
  • Venmo

Online payment isn't set up yet — for now, fees are handled the same way as always: cash, check, or Venmo, in person or through your twirler's folder.

Need more time?

If you need more time to make a payment, please speak to Miss Heidi Sue in advance. Arrangements can be made — just ask before the due date.

Also worth knowing

Competition entry fees

Parents are responsible for competition entry fees, which are invoiced separately from team fees.

Small incidentals

Occasional small charges — music CDs, supplies — may come up during the season.

Fundraising is optional

A few optional fundraisers are offered throughout the year to help offset costs.
