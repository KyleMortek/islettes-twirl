Common questions

What age can my child start?

Starlettes takes twirlers in Pre-K and Kindergarten. From there they move through Shooting Stars, Rising Stars, and Starfire — all the way through college.

How is my twirler placed?

Units are based on age and ability. Get in touch and we'll help find the right starting point.

What does it cost?

There's a registration fee, monthly fees, and occasional costume or fundraiser costs. See the fees page for the full picture.

How much of a commitment is it?

Attendance at all competitions, performances, and parades is expected, and twirlers practise at home between sessions. The handbook lays out exactly what a season looks like.
