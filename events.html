Event day, step by step

The same routine every time, so families know exactly what to expect.

  1. 1. Check in

    On arrival, find Miss Heidi Sue or Miss Molly to check your child in. Each staff member supervises a group of twirlers, and Starfire members assist their younger “sisters.”

  2. 2. Head to the bleachers

    Once your child is checked in, please head to the bleachers or the parade viewing area. Staff handle warm-ups and will send twirlers back to you between events if time allows.

  3. 3. Watch and cheer

    Please stay in designated areas and enjoy all the performances. Times are approximate — exact schedules can't be guaranteed.

  4. 4. Stay to the end

    All twirlers are expected to remain until the team's final performance, then they're released by Miss Molly or Miss Heidi Sue.

  5. 5. Awards come later

    Awards are typically handed out at a later celebration — a pizza party, for example — where every participating twirler receives a ribbon.

  6. In an emergency

    Miss Molly is the primary contact when Miss Heidi Sue is judging. Twirlers should stay together throughout the event.

The big rules

No flash photography

No flash photography at competitions, ever — it's a safety issue for athletes mid-toss.

Film our team only

You may only video our team's performances.

Don't compare teams

Every team has its own goals and training focus. We cheer loudly for our girls and always applaud everyone else.

Missing more than 25%

If a twirler misses more than 25% of events, they may be removed from the team. Missing a competition puts stress on the whole group.

Parades

How parade day works

Twirlers perform throughout the parade route. We provide labelled water bottles, staff monitor hydration, and staff are positioned throughout the group to keep everyone safe.

At the end of the route, twirlers gather in a circle — youngest in the centre, surrounded by the older girls. Pickup happens only when a staff member personally releases your child to you. Please don't step into the street or approach your twirler before then. We hold a brief, positive team chat before dismissal.

Want to walk with us?

Join our Booster Group. You'll need the ’25–’26 Team Shirt, red or black bottoms, and sneakers, and you'll walk behind the team.

Transportation trouble?

Other parents are often happy to help. Let Miss Heidi Sue know as soon as you're aware of a possible conflict so we can plan around it.
