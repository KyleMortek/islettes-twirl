Our units

Four units, Pre-K through college

Islettes' Elite places twirlers by age and ability. Every unit competes in local NBTA and Twirling Unlimited competitions, and marches in local parades and performances.

Find your twirler's unit

Placement is by grade and ability. Each unit has its own practice dress code, and all of them follow the same rule: it isn't optional.

Starlettes

Pre-K & Kindergarten

Our youngest twirlers. Starlettes learn the fundamentals of baton and movement, and perform alongside the whole team at local parades and competitions.

Practice dress code

  • ShoesSneakers (no high tops)
  • BottomsBlack leggings
  • Top’25–’26 Team T-Shirt (grey)
  • HairUp in a ponytail — mandatory at every practice

Bring a bottle of water to every practice. If it's cold, wear the ’25–’26 Team Sweatshirt or a solid grey sweatshirt.

Dress code is not optional

Team members and staff must always follow the dress code. Twirlers who arrive with improper attire or hair may be asked to sit out and observe.

What we expect at practice

Come ready to work

Arrive prepared and free from distractions. Each class is an opportunity to grow and improve at something you love.

Practise at home

Beyond scheduled practices, twirlers work independently at home. Come to team practice with the previous week's material already memorised.

Set your own goals

Individual twirlers dedicate additional practice time based on their personal goals. Your results reflect the time and commitment you devote to baton twirling.

Drop-off is encouraged

Athletes often concentrate better when parents aren't present. You're welcome to drop off and return at the end of the session, and we ask that class isn't interrupted with questions or coaching suggestions.

Report absences ahead of time

Let Miss Heidi Sue know before class starts. Twirlers who miss a class should check with teammates or staff to review the material — only brief reviews are given in following classes.

Injured? Come anyway

Twirlers who are ill but not contagious, or who are injured, should still attend practice to observe.

Not sure which unit fits?

Placement is based on grade and ability. Get in touch and we'll help you find the right starting point for your twirler.
