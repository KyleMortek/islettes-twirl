Team structure

Islettes' Elite offers four units based on age and ability:

  • Starlettes — Pre-K and Kindergarten
  • Shooting Stars — 1st Grade
  • Rising Stars — 2nd to 5th Grade
  • Starfire — 6th Grade through College

All units of the team participate in local NBTA and TU competitions, as well as local parades and performances.

Practice guidelines & expectations

Athletes often concentrate better if parents are not present. Parents may drop off their child and return at the end of the session. Please avoid interrupting class with questions or coaching suggestions.

In addition to scheduled practices, athletes are expected to practise independently at home. During team practices, twirlers should have material memorised from the previous week.

Individual twirlers must dedicate additional practice time based on their personal goals. Remember: your results reflect the time and commitment you devote to baton twirling.

Dress code

Team members and staff must always follow the dress code. Twirlers who arrive with improper attire or hair may be asked to sit out and observe.

Starlettes, Shooting Stars & Rising Stars

  • Sneakers (no high tops)
  • Black leggings
  • ’25–’26 Team T-Shirt (grey)
  • Hair up in a ponytail — absolutely mandatory at every practice

Starfire

  • Tan jazz shoes
  • Black leggings
  • ’25–’26 Team T-Shirt (grey)
  • Hair up in a ponytail — absolutely mandatory at every practice

If twirlers are cold, they may wear the ’25–’26 Team Sweatshirt or a solid grey sweatshirt. All twirlers should bring bottled water to every practice.

Practice attendance

The first step to becoming a successful twirler is making a commitment to attend all classes, performances, and competitions.

Come prepared to learn — free from distractions. Each class is an opportunity to grow and improve at what you love.

  • Report absences by text to Miss Heidi Sue prior to the start of class.
  • Twirlers who miss class should check with teammates or staff to review missed material and choreography. Only brief reviews will be given during subsequent classes.
  • Habitual absences may result in removal from certain sections of choreography or routines.
  • Twirlers who are ill but not contagious, or who are injured, should still attend practice to observe.

Costumes

All group costumes are selected by Miss Heidi Sue and Miss Molly. Twirlers must wear all parts of the costume as specified, including tights, shoes, makeup, and hair accessories.

We strive to keep costs as low as possible, and we do not profit from any costume or clothing purchases.

Competitions, performances & parades

Attendance at all competitions, performances, and parades is expected. A calendar is distributed monthly, including any newly confirmed dates. Missing a competition places stress on the team and may limit routine adaptability.

If a twirler misses more than 25% of events, they may be removed from the team.

If transportation is an issue, other parents are often happy to assist. Please notify Miss Heidi Sue as soon as you know of a potential conflict so we can plan accordingly.

Event procedures

Upon arrival, locate Miss Heidi Sue or Miss Molly to check your child in. Each staff member supervises a group of twirlers (Miss Molly, Miss Anna, and Miss Alexi), and Starfire members assist their younger “sisters.”

Once your child is checked in, parents should proceed to the bleachers or parade route viewing area. If you wish to walk with the team in a parade, you must wear the ’25–’26 Team Shirt, red or black bottoms, and sneakers — you'll walk behind the team as part of our Booster Group.

During performances or competitions, parents may sit in the bleachers. Staff handle warm-ups and send twirlers back to you between events if time allows. Please remain in designated areas and enjoy all performances.

Big rules

  • No flash photography at competitions.
  • You may only video our team's performances.
  • Please avoid comparing our team to others — each has its own goals and training focus. Islettes' Elite has proudly earned over 20 National Titles, and every generation brings its own strengths and spirit.
  • We cheer loudly for our girls and always applaud other teams, modelling Christian sportsmanship and positivity.

Twirlers should stay together throughout each event. Times are approximate; exact schedules cannot be guaranteed. If an emergency arises, Miss Molly is the primary contact when Miss Heidi Sue is judging.

All twirlers are expected to remain until the team's final performance. Once finished, they'll be released by Miss Molly or Miss Heidi Sue. Awards are typically distributed at a later celebration (for example, a pizza party), where every participating twirler receives a ribbon.

Parades

Twirlers perform throughout the parade route. Labelled water bottles are provided, and staff monitor hydration. Staff are positioned throughout the group to ensure safety.

At the end of the parade, twirlers gather in a circle — youngest in the centre, surrounded by the older “big girls.” Pickup occurs only when staff personally release your child to you. Please do not enter the street or approach your child before release. We also hold a brief, positive team chat before dismissal.

Your child's safety is always our top priority.

Fees & payment

  • Registration fees — due at the first attended class.
  • Monthly fees — an envelope is provided monthly; payment is due by the second practice of the next month.
  • Fundraiser payments — due within two weeks of the fundraiser's closing date.
  • Other fees — may be broken into payments, but must be paid before receiving any purchased items.

If you need more time to make a payment, please speak to Miss Heidi Sue in advance — arrangements can be made.

Parents are responsible for competition entry fees, which are invoiced separately. Occasional small charges (for example, CDs or supplies) may also apply.

We accept cash, checks, and Venmo.

Fundraising: a few optional fundraisers are offered throughout the year.

Behaviour

Members of Islettes' Elite are ambassadors of WNY. Twirlers and their families must model positive behaviour at all times — at the gym, at events, at competitions, and online.

We do not tolerate bullying, gossip, foul language, or discrimination. We expect everyone — twirlers and parents — to support a healthy, respectful environment. Any member or parent who cannot uphold these standards may be removed from the team.

Communication

Most communication occurs via:

  • Your child's team folder
  • Text message
  • The Remind app, for timely updates

Please check your child's folder weekly.

Commitment

Being part of Islettes' Elite is a family commitment. Time and dedication are key to each twirler's success. Families are encouraged to stay engaged and supportive throughout the season.

Twirler's commitment

  • Arrive prepared, properly dressed, and ready to work
  • Maintain good attendance
  • Practise at home — stretching, skills, routines
  • Keep your team folder organised and bring it to every practice
  • Stay positive — you can do hard things!
  • Show respect and support for all teammates
  • Remember: practice = progress

Parent's commitment

  • Bring your twirler prepared and on time
  • Encourage practice at home
  • Stay informed — check the team folder after every practice
  • Be an active supporter, including the Parent Support Team for parades
  • Show respect, support, and kindness to all members
  • Be your twirler's biggest fan!
